Good to know

Make the road easy.

Small things make a big difference in the hills—especially when the weather changes its mind.

☁

Weather wins

Check live rain and landslide advisories before hill drives. Avoid night driving on ghat roads whenever possible.

⌁

Plan for bends

Carry motion-sickness tablets if needed. Give the driver breaks and keep the co-pilot focused on navigation.

⌂

Book ahead

Reserve accommodation and special park access early for weekends and holiday periods. Ask hotels about parking.
